## How to read this

- **Alarm level (0-5):** 0 = context/procedural, 1 = notable, 2 = concerning, 3 = serious, 4 = severe (a clear conflict, a policy with mass-harm consequences, or a sanction on accountability bodies), 5 = extreme (mass-casualty or famine outcome, war entry, or a direct financial benefit flowing to an official from a foreign government with business before the US).
- **[BG]** = pre-term background (before 2025-01-20). Use it for context, not as a backfilled "new" story.
- **[UNVERIFIED]** = the date or fact came from secondary or summary sources only, or no URL was retrieved this session. Confirm it before publishing.
- **[URL to confirm]** = the event is well established, but this session could not get a working URL from a reputable outlet. AP, Reuters, NYT and the Guardian block the research crawler. Their URLs appear only where another page cited them verbatim.
- **Attribution rule for the Israel front:** genocide findings are always written as "X concludes / says". Never in the site's own voice. Israel's government (ministries, PM's office, FARA-registered contracts) is kept separate from US-based pro-Israel organizations (AIPAC, UDP, DMFI) and from individual US donors.
